# BandLab+

A free browser extension that adds what BandLab Studio is missing: full themes, way more track colours, a free master EQ, better monitoring, editing fixes, custom preset icons and one-click publishing to Genius.

**Download and info:** https://quoats.xyz/#bandlab

Made by [Quoats](https://quoats.xyz). BandLab+ is an independent fan-made extension. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by BandLab.

## Features

- **Full Studio themes**: restyle all of BandLab Studio with complete themes, or build your own in the theme editor (save, share, export, import). It loads before the page draws, so the stock look never flashes.
- **Way more track colours**: a 10-column palette (vivid, pastel, deep, neutral, plus a colour picker) that recolours each track's header, clips and waveform. Waveforms are only tinted, so they stay exactly in line with the audio.
- **Free Master EQ**: Low, Mid and High knobs (plus the Mid frequency) on the Mastering tab, built from BandLab's free 3-Band EQ at the end of every track. It's saved in the project, so exports and releases include it, and each change is one undo step.
- **Better monitoring**: a Monitor button with its own volume and an FX switch. Hear your track's effects, or your raw mic without effects (recordings still keep them). You can also set a default mic and input channels for every track.
- **Editing that doesn't get stuck**: unsticks BandLab when Slice or Remove Takes stop responding, with no reload needed, and pauses clicks while a save runs so saves stop failing halfway.
- **Custom preset icons**: design a colour icon (pattern, colour, short label) right in the Save Preset box.
- **Publish to Genius**: gathers your track's title, cover, lyrics, release date and album, then fills in Genius's Add A Song form and the song details for you. Stylised names like "Råce ✓" still match "Race". You press Submit yourself, on your own account.

## Install

1. Download the zip from https://quoats.xyz/#bandlab (or **Code → Download ZIP** here) and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`) and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the **Bandlab+** folder (the one with `manifest.json` in it).
4. Open BandLab Studio. The **Bandlab+** toolbar icon opens themes and settings.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera on desktop.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no tracking, nothing sent to any server. Genius publishing uses your own Genius login, and you submit the song yourself.
